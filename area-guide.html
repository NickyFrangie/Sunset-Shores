Area Activities

Welcome to Carpinteria

Park your car in our gated parking lot, because the best way to see and enjoy Carpinteria is by bike or on foot. The downtown shopping area includes restaurants, art galleries, antique shops, a supermarket, and a weekly Farmers Market featuring locally grown produce. An electric trolley stops directly in front of Sunset Shores, and the Amtrak train station, offering scenic train travel along the ocean to Santa Barbara and Ventura, is within walking distance. Visit the Harbor Seal Sanctuary or take a tour of the Salt Marsh Wetlands — there's always something great to do in and around Carpinteria.

Downtown Carpinteria
A 5-minute walk to restaurants, art galleries, antique shops, and a full supermarket — all walkable from Sunset Shores.
Weekly Farmers Market
Fresh, locally grown produce, flowers, and artisan goods sold right in the heart of downtown every week.
Amtrak & Electric Trolley
An electric trolley stops right in front of Sunset Shores, and the Amtrak station offers scenic coastal rail travel to Santa Barbara and Ventura.

Right Next Door

Wildlife & Natural Wonders

Harbor Seal SanctuaryWatch wild harbor seals rest and birth their pups along Carpinteria's protected coastline.
Salt Marsh PreserveWalk the trails of this protected wetland sanctuary, home to over 200 species of birds.
Carpinteria State Beach Tide PoolsExplore tide pools teeming with starfish, anemones, and crabs at low tide.
Monarch Butterfly HabitatVisit during winter months to see thousands of monarch butterflies clustered in the eucalyptus grove.
Carpinteria State Beach Park Visitor CenterStop by for maps, exhibits, and ranger-led programs about the local ecosystem.
Whale WatchingCarpinteria sits along the migration path for gray whales, humpbacks, and even blue whales.
