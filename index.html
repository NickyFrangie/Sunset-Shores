About Sunset Shores

A coastal retreat
like no other

Sunset Shores is a collection of fully furnished condominiums perched at the edge of Carpinteria's beloved state beach. Every unit comes stocked with everything you need to feel completely at home the moment you arrive.

Swim year-round in the heated pool, unwind in the whirlpool spa, or take in sweeping views of the Santa Ynez Mountains with the Pacific at your back. The Amtrak station is two blocks away; downtown shops, restaurants, and a weekly farmers market are a short stroll.
