How do I make a reservation?
You can book online for rentals of one or two weeks, or call us for longer rentals at (805) 684-3682 or email us. We look forward to hearing from you!
Can I rent a condo for one night?
No, we have a seven night minimum stay requirement on all reservations.
What are the rates?
The rates change depending on the season, the size of the unit, and the length of stay. Check our Rates & Reservations page for more information, or contact us directly.
Do you offer AAA, senior, or other discounts?
Because each condo is individually owned and priced, we're unable to offer these types of discounts.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards. A $500 refundable security deposit is required to hold your reservation, and rent is due 30 days prior to arrival.
Do you have an elevator?
No, we do not have an elevator. However, we do have luggage carts available to help you move your belongings to your condo with ease.
What should I bring?
All condos are fully furnished and have fully stocked kitchens. All come with bed covers, blankets, and pillows. If you'd like us to also provide bed sheets and bath towels (for an extra charge), please let us know in advance. Otherwise, please save some space in your luggage for those items.
How many parking spaces are available for each condo?
Each condo has one parking space inside our gated lot. There's also free street parking in front of our complex with no time limit, except on Tuesdays between 9:00am and 11:00am when street sweeping occurs.
Do all vacation rental condos at Sunset Shores have ocean views?
Some do and some don't. Those that do only have partial views, since we're located across the street from the beach and not directly on the beach.
Are there barbecues available for use?
Yes, natural gas barbecues are located in the common areas of each building. We also have full-sized picnic tables for you to use.
Please be advised that barbecues are not allowed on private patios or decks.
Do condos at Sunset Shores ever come up for sale?
Yes, but we are strictly a rental office, so you'll need to contact a local real estate agent if you're interested in purchasing a unit.
Can I bring my pet with me?
Sorry, no pets are allowed. Service animals specifically trained to aid a person with a disability are welcome.
Can we have guests come to visit?
Yes, but remember a maximum of only four to six people (depending on the condo) can stay overnight in a condo on any given night. The person whose name the reservation is in must be present for any overnight guests, unless previous arrangements are made with management. No subletting is allowed.
Is smoking permitted at Sunset Shores?
No, Sunset Shores is a smoke-free property.
Smoking is not allowed anywhere on our grounds, and the City of Carpinteria has a no smoking ordinance that is strictly enforced.
What is your cancellation policy?
All cancelled reservations are subject to a $250 cancellation fee. If you cancel less than 60 days prior to arrival, a $500 cancellation fee will apply. No refunds will be issued for reservations cancelled less than 30 days prior to arrival, unless the unit is re-rented for the same time period and rate. Full details are on our Rates & Reservations page.
What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-out time is 10:00am. To avoid cleaning charges beyond your prepaid departure cleaning fee, please leave the unit as you found it at check-in. See our Rental Policies page for full check-in and check-out guidelines.
