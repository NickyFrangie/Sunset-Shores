Large Studio
Sleeps a maximum of three people. This unit has a sleeping alcove furnished with a queen size bed and a sofa sleeper in the living room.
Deluxe Studio
Sleeps a maximum of three people. This unit has a bedroom with a queen size bed, a sofa sleeper in the living room, and features either a private ocean view balcony or a private patio.
One Bedroom
Sleeps a maximum of four people. This unit has a queen size bed in the bedroom and a sofa sleeper in the living room.
Large One Bedroom
Sleeps a maximum of four people. This unit has a queen size bed in the bedroom and a sofa sleeper in the living room. Provides more living space than a One Bedroom and features a larger kitchen with separate dining area. Some have private patios as well.
Two Bedrooms
Sleeps a maximum of six people. One bedroom has a queen size bed, one bedroom has twin beds, and the living room has a queen size sofa sleeper.
